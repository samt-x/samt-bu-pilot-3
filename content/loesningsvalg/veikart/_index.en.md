The pilot period runs until the end of November 2026, and capacity until then is limited. The pilot is planning and estimating a possible continuation in 2027, with the goal of an MVP for an AI model that categorises the documents in the pupil file. No continuation has been decided.

## Three steps

![Phases in a possible follow-up of Pilot 3: 1 legal assessment of legal basis for processing and privacy, 2 preparation of training data, either synthetic data or collected and anonymised real data, 3 setup and training of a model as an MVP or POC, with classical machine learning and not LLM (slide in Norwegian)](faser-videreforing.png)

1. **Legal:** assessment of the legal basis for processing and of privacy, both for training the model and for using it. The pilot is waiting for an answer from the Norwegian Tax Administration (Skatteetaten) on whether it has legal assessments that can be reused. The scope of this step is uncertain.
2. **Training data:** either synthetic data or collected and anonymised real data. Steps 1 and 2 can partly run in parallel.
3. **Technical:** setup and training of the model as an MVP or POC, with classical machine learning.

The estimate assumes synthetic test data in KS Digital's sandbox, and that the result is the categorisation shown in a console.

## Resource needs

The estimate covers three months (65 working days):

| Role | Task | Share |
|---|---|---|
| AI adviser, technical | Setup and training of the model | 50 % |
| Technical resource, data | Making synthetic training data | 30 % |
| Lawyer | Assessing the legal basis for processing | 50 % |
| Domain expert (external) | Helping with categories, test data and training | 30 % |
| Architect | Overall architecture | 20 % |
| Project manager | Project management, coordination and reporting | 50 % |

## Before a continuation

- define precisely what a POC or MVP shall deliver
- bring in school staff who can help with the categories, first in the municipalities that took part in the workshops
- obtain technical AI resources, which the pilot has not had access to in 2026
- clarify whether real pupil files can be used, and on what legal and formal basis
- in the longer term: whether the solution should be made by the supplier market
