Who is involved when a pupil moves during the school year, which use case the pilot has used, and what the different actors need.

## Use case

The workshops took as their starting point that **a pupil moves from Oslo to Nittedal**. With the City of Oslo, the pilot looked in particular at what happens when the pupil moves out. With Nittedal municipality, it looked in particular at what happens when the pupil moves in, but also at the process as a whole. See [Current situation](/en/pilotering/pilot-3/naasituasjon/).

## Actors

| Actor | Role in the move |
|---|---|
| Pupil and parents | Move and register the move in the new municipality or county authority. The parents consent to information that requires it being sent to the new school |
| The school the pupil moves from | Closes the pupil relationship, obtains consent, selects documents and sends the information |
| The school the pupil moves to | Receives the information, registers it and uses it to plan the education |
| School administration systems | Hold standard information about the pupil, such as grades and absence (Visma Flyt Skole, IST and EG Vigilo in the municipalities, Visma InSchool in the county authorities) |
| Case and archive systems | Hold the pupil file with documents about the pupil (Acos WebSak, Sikri Elements, Documaster and Tieto Public 360) |
| Fiks SvarUt and SvarInn | KS Digital's services for sending and receiving the shipments |
| The National Population Register | Registers the move |

## Needs

- **The new school** quickly needs information that is relevant to the education, especially whether the pupil needs adaptations. It needs a focused selection, not the whole pupil file. It would prefer to get the information into its own systems without typing it in again.
- **The school the pupil moves from** needs help to decide which documents can and should be sent, and which require consent. For some pupils there are hundreds of documents.
- **Parents** with limited language skills or low digital skills need consent solutions and information they understand. Today this can require a lot of follow-up from teachers and school administration.
- **The municipalities** ask for common routines and best practice. They want better workflows in the systems they already have, not more digital solutions on top.
- **The pupil** is entitled to correct information, to only what is necessary being shared, and to old information not following the pupil longer than necessary.
